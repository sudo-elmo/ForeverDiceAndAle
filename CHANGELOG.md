# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Lobby codes and playing without a group now work on Forever: each lobby gets its own hidden chat channel for all multiplayer messages.
- You can leave the group at any time, even mid-game.
- After `/reload` or relogging, a running game comes back by itself.
- Joining shows "Connecting to …" until the host lets you in, and tells you what to check if nobody answers.
- New command `/dna status` to help with multiplayer problems.
- Fixed old player names showing in the Multiplayer window, and windows being see-through.
- Everyone needs 1.1.0.

## 1.0.5

- Lobby codes: host a game without a group and share a code like `Anna-4821` (`/dna join Anna-4821`).
- The Roll button is disabled when all five dice are held.
- Fixed a broken symbol in a settings tooltip.

## 1.0.4

- The settings gear no longer locks up the screen in combat.

## 1.0.3

- The window stays open on flight paths.
- New settings page (`/diceale options`, or the gear next to the close button): Escape behaviour, flights, dice and "your turn" sounds, auto-open on your turn, invitation popup.

## 1.0.2

- Multiplayer recovers from `/reload`, disconnects and missed messages.
- Players gone for over a minute are skipped and can rejoin later.
- The game goes on when someone leaves.
- Finishing a multiplayer game no longer deletes your solo game.

## 1.0.1

- Fixed players not seeing each other's lobby or moves.
- Lost messages are resent.

## 1.0.0

- First release: the classic five-dice game, solo or with 2 to 6 players in your group, in English and German.
